Understanding Business Structures

The way you structure your entities affects privacy, risk, and tax outcomes. Here are some of the most common building blocks we use for Wyoming-based strategies.

Holding Companies

Use a parent entity to own multiple operating LLCs, bank accounts, or real estate, creating a central control point with stronger separation.

Series LLCs

Create multiple protected “cells” under one umbrella LLC structure, separating different projects, portfolios, or properties from each other.

Asset Protection

Separate business entities reduce spillover risk from lawsuits or creditors, keeping personal homes, savings, and unrelated ventures insulated.

Tax Efficiency

Align entities with how you want to be taxed—disregarded, partnership, or S-corp election—to balance liability protection and tax optimization.
